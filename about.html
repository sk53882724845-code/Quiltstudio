ABOUT PIXORA

Creative work should be clear, useful and honest.

Pixora Studio is a small independent creative-studio concept focused on digital graphics and visual communication.

Our approach

We believe strong design begins with understanding the message. Before choosing colours or effects, we consider who the design is for, where it will appear and what the viewer should understand.

We use accessible design practices: readable text, sensible contrast, clear spacing and layouts suited to their intended screens.

Studio principles

Clarity
Make the message easy to understand.

Honesty
Represent experience, samples and deliverables accurately.

Care
Respect rights, privacy and project requirements.
