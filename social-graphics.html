Choosing the right size for social graphics

A well-designed graphic can still look wrong if it is prepared for the wrong placement. Start by identifying where it will appear, then verify the current platform requirements.

Start with the placement

“Social media image” is not one format. A feed post, vertical story, profile cover and video thumbnail can all use different proportions. Decide the placement before creating the document.

Check current specifications

Social platforms update their interfaces and recommendations. Check the platform's own help or creator documentation for current dimensions, accepted file types and file-size limits. Avoid relying on an old size chart without checking it.

Keep important content in a safe area

Interface elements may cover parts of a design. Keep headlines, logos and essential information away from the outer edges, and preview the design in the intended placement where possible.

Design for small screens

Use a clear headline, strong contrast and a simple reading order. Tiny paragraphs and decorative fonts can become difficult to read on a phone.

Export and inspect

Choose an appropriate image format and quality setting. Open the exported file on a phone and check that text remains readable, edges are not clipped and colours look consistent.

Quick checklist

  • Correct platform and placement
  • Current dimensions verified
  • Readable text and sufficient contrast
  • Important elements clear of edges
  • Export inspected on a phone
  • Image rights and permissions checked
This guide is general educational information. Platform requirements and project needs can change; check the relevant platform's current documentation before publishing.
